ABOUT ME

Engineering starts with curiosity.

I'm an aerospace engineering student at Iowa State University who enjoys building, testing, and improving things, especially aircraft and engineering systems.

BACKGROUND

Learning by doing

My interest in engineering goes beyond the classroom. Building and flying RC aircraft has helped me explore how structures, electronics, and flight characteristics work together. Some of the most valuable lessons have come from troubleshooting what did not work as expected.

I'm particularly interested in aircraft systems, design, and research. I hope to build on my experience through internships and projects that challenge me to apply what I'm learning.

BEYOND ENGINEERING

Interests and involvement

Outside my coursework, I enjoy RC aviation, FPV drones, software development, and music. Employment, volunteering, and student activities have also helped me develop leadership, communication, and teamwork skills.
